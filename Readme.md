## This music player embodies simplicity. In fact, it is so simple, I can't use big words to describe it.

It is folder centric, that is, there are NO playlists, NO favorites, No shuffle, and NO huge set of 
confusing and poorly explained options or settings.

In fact, there are two settings: What is the root of your local music collection on your phone? 
and, do you want it to resume at the last point when restarted ?

That is all ! If you're like me, your eyes probably gloss over when you have to navigate 100 options, 
all of which have very little backing information, and even when they have some of that - who can understand it ?

Here, all you are doing is selecting folders and files within them, just like you would navigate any 
folder oriented file structure on your computer. Presumably you've organized your music in 
physical folders for one reason or another - this system trusts that whatever reason you had - it was a good one.
